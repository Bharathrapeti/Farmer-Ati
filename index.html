About FARMER ATI

A Smart Agricultural & Hydroponic Information System built on an ESP32 microcontroller with a 128×64 OLED display.

  • 📡 Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) live mirroring
  • 🌿 10-crop agronomy database
  • 🐛 10 common farmer problem guides
  • 🧮 Seed rate & tree calculators
  • 📅 Month-based crop calendar
  • 🏫 Kendriya Vidyalaya No.2 · NCSC 2026–27
