About FARMER ATI

A comprehensive Smart Agricultural & Hydroponic Information System developed with an ESP32 microcontroller, SH1106 OLED display, and companion dashboard for Android tablets and smartphones.

Key System Capabilities & Features

  • 🌾 Continuous Agricultural Weather Advisory: Real-time pesticide spray suitability ticker driven by live atmospheric telemetry.
  • 🌦️ Live Weather Telemetry: Automatic tablet GPS geolocation, precipitation probability, humidity, and temperature forecasting via Open-Meteo.
  • 💧 Intelligent Farm Pond Telemetry: 1-Acre farm pond model (50× scale) with ultrasonic water level sensing, storage capacity analytics, and automated seasonal crop recommendations.
  • 📡 Dual-Screen BLE Mirroring: Physical 4×4 keypad-driven navigation with real-time Bluetooth Low Energy tablet synchronization.
  • 🌿 Comprehensive Agronomy Library: 10 staple & commercial crops with complete agronomy guides and hydroponic parameters.
  • 🐛 Farmer Problem Solutions: 10 practical field pest, disease, and soil management guides with actionable remedies.
  • 🧮 Intelligent Planting Calculator: Crop-specific requirement modeling for seeds (kg), trees, suckers, setts, and seedlings based on TNAU agronomic rates.
  • 🌱 Nutrient Care Guide: Complete visual deficiency diagnosis across 11 primary, secondary, and micronutrients.

Remember — Golden Agronomic Rules for Farm Nutrition

  • Balanced Nutrition = Healthy Plant: Nitrogen builds the vegetative frame, Phosphorus drives root and flower vigor, and Potassium guards against drought, pests, and disease.
  • Law of the Minimum (Liebig's Law): Plant growth is dictated by the scarcest nutrient (limiting factor). Adding excess Urea cannot compensate for a Zinc or Boron deficiency.
  • Too Little or Too Much is Harmful: Over-application of Nitrogen invites succulent stems and fungal pests; excessive Potassium can lock out Magnesium and Calcium.
  • Soil Testing & Targeted Foliar Feeding: Conduct routine soil tests to know your baseline. When quick correction is required during peak vegetative or fruit development, foliar micronutrient sprays give rapid results.
